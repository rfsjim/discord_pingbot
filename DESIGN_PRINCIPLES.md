# Design Principles #

**The bot should reduce social friction, not become another source of it**

## Guiding Principles
- Courtesy over enforcement. Remind people of preferences without treating them as offenders.
- Clarity over decoration. Plain text is the default; richer interfaces need a reason to exist.
- Respect without explanation. Users can express a notification preference without disclosing why they have it.
- Minimum necessary interruption. One consolidated reminder is better than a response for every mentioned member.
- Assume good intentions. An unnecessary mention isn't automatically malicious.
- Accessibility by design. Consider different communication and sensory preferences from the beginning, rather than treating them as exceptional cases.
- Complexity must earn its place. A feature should solve a genuine problem, not merely demonstrate what discord.js can do.
